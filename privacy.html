This policy describes the public website at getaurify.com and inquiries sent to hi@getaurify.com. Aurify is operated by Getaurify Inc.

When you visit this website

The website does not include analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms, or tracking cookies. The product preview uses fictional sample data. Viewing it does not connect your accounts or send information to an AI agent.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process technical information about requests, such as IP addresses and browser information, to operate and protect its services. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

When you contact us

If you email us, you share your email address and any name, business details, or message content you include. We use that information to respond and handle your inquiry. Email is processed through the providers involved in sending and receiving it.

Questions about your information

To ask about information you have shared, or request a correction or deletion, email hi@getaurify.com. Please do not send customer records or sensitive information when making an initial inquiry.

Product data

This website policy does not describe data processing inside a customer deployment of Aurify. Product access, connected accounts, and customer data require the applicable service agreement and privacy information for that deployment.
