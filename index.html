AI agents for local businesses, starting with home-service teams. Help with answering leads, following up, and booking the next job while you’re busy doing the work.

Aurify is a SaaS platform designed around an AI front office, with Claude at its core. The idea is simple: give agents the customer records and conversation history they need, so every inquiry has the right context behind it.

Let’s talk ↗

The work we’re here for

From first hello to the next job.

Our focus is contractors and home-service teams. Here’s the everyday work our AI agents are designed to help with.

01 Answer new leads
Respond to inbound inquiries over SMS and web chat, and answer questions using your services, service area, and business information. Include a text-back after a missed call.
02 Qualify the job
Ask the relevant questions, understand what the customer needs, and gather details so your team can decide whether the job is a good fit.
03 Follow up
Keep track of conversations that need another reply. Help customers take the next step when a quote or inquiry hasn’t turned into a booking.
04 Reconnect
Revisit old opportunities and reconnect with previous customers through relevant messages, where they’ve agreed to hear from your business.
05 Book the next step
Work toward appointment booking through connected scheduling tools, within the availability and rules your team sets.
06 Keep the context
Use CRM records and conversation history to suggest the next useful action, update customer information, or hand the conversation to your team.

Our wider scope covers genuine Google review requests and response drafting, Google Business Profile support, websites, and local SEO. We don’t write, buy, or filter customer reviews.

The approach

AI agents, with the right context.

Aurify’s agent architecture is designed around Anthropic’s Claude API, business information, customer records (CRM), and past conversations. The goal is to understand the request and work out the next useful step.

The approach combines Claude with tool calling across CRM, messaging, and scheduling. These connections give agents a way to check availability, update a customer record, or pass a conversation to the right person.

Your business sets the boundaries: which messages and actions need review, what an agent can handle, and when it should bring you in. Accurate answers, reliable actions, and clear handoffs are central to the design.
