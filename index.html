The work we’re here for

From first hello to the next job.

We’re starting with contractors and home-service teams. Here’s the everyday work we’re building our agents to help with.

01 Answer new leads
Respond to inbound inquiries over SMS and web chat, and answer questions using your services, service area, and business information. Our planned workflows also include a text-back after a missed call.
02 Qualify the job
Ask the relevant questions, understand what the customer needs, and gather details so your team can decide whether the job is a good fit.
03 Follow up
Keep track of conversations that need another reply. Help customers take the next step when a quote or inquiry hasn’t turned into a booking.
04 Reconnect
Revisit old opportunities and reconnect with previous customers through relevant messages, where they’ve agreed to hear from your business.
05 Book the next step
Work toward appointment booking through connected scheduling tools, within the availability and rules your team sets.
06 Keep the context
Use CRM records and conversation history to suggest the next useful action, update customer information, or hand the conversation to your team.

Alongside the front office, our planned services include genuine Google review requests and response drafting, Google Business Profile support, websites, and local SEO. We don’t write, buy, or filter customer reviews.

How we’re building it

AI agents, with the right context.

We’re building around Anthropic’s Claude API. The agent will use your business information, customer records (CRM), and past conversations to understand a request and work out the next useful step.

Our planned agent workflows use tool calling to connect Claude to CRM, messaging, and scheduling tools. That’s how an agent will check availability or update a customer record, rather than just write a reply.

We’re starting with messages and actions you review first. As the service develops, you’ll set what the agent can handle on its own and when it should bring you in. Before launch, we’ll test for accurate answers, reliable actions, and a clear handoff when the agent needs help.

A roofing inquiry, for example.

Illustrative workflow · Planned functionality

  1. A customer asks about a roof repair. The agent checks the business’s services and existing CRM context.
  2. Claude helps answer their questions and gather the job details, such as location, issue, and preferred timing.
  3. Within the owner’s rules and approvals, the agent checks appointment availability, helps arrange the next step, and records the conversation in the CRM.
