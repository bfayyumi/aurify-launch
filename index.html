The customer journey

From new lead to booked job.

Aurify’s workflows are designed to handle the repetitive work between a customer’s first message and the next appointment.

Inquiry → Conversation → Qualification → Follow-up → Booking → CRM

  1. Respond

    Answer new leads over SMS and web chat using the business’s services, service area, FAQs, and operating information.

  2. Qualify

    Understand what the customer needs and collect the details required to decide whether the opportunity is a fit.

  3. Follow up

    Continue eligible conversations when an inquiry, estimate, or opportunity hasn’t moved forward, within the business’s follow-up rules.

  4. Reactivate

    Reconnect with eligible past leads and customers using CRM history and previous conversation context.

  5. Book

    Check connected calendars and arrange appointments around the team’s availability and scheduling rules.

  6. Update the CRM

    Keep contact records, conversation context, lead status, and next actions together as the conversation progresses.

The reasoning layer

Built on Claude.

Claude is the reasoning and language layer behind Aurify’s AI agents. The architecture brings together business information, CRM records, and conversation history to understand a customer request and determine the next appropriate action.

Through tool calling, the agents are designed to work with CRM, messaging, and scheduling systems: checking availability, updating records, arranging appointments, continuing conversations, or handing them to a person.

The business sets what agents can do on their own, what needs approval, and when a conversation should be escalated.

Claude provides the reasoning. Aurify provides the business context, integrations, workflow rules, and actions around it.
